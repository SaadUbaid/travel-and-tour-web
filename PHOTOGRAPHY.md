## Film

Attabad Lake aerial footage by **Abdul Ahad**, [Pexels source](https://www.pexels.com/video/aerial-footage-of-a-valley-7748528/), [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The site uses a silent 19-second excerpt beginning at 68 seconds, re-encoded to 1920px and 1280px H.264, plus a poster frame from the same footage. The unmodified scene depicts Attabad Lake; the supplied stylized reference video is not used.
